## Bug fixed during the run

**Reader did not resume** (`apps/web/components/bible/BibleReader.tsx`, commit `17bec40`). Without a link, the reader always opened Genesis 1 in the app language and recorded it, overwriting the saved position. Switching Bible language also jumped to Genesis 1. The reader now waits for the saved position before loading a chapter, and keeps the current chapter across a language switch. Verified in the browser and in the history table.
