# M3.8 — Ready for public launch

Started 18 October 2026. **Goal:** LifeBook can be opened to the public in Ghana and France with production sign-in, an honest privacy notice, reviewed pastoral content, and a signed-in production test that passes.

Track progress with one command (read-only, no secrets):

```bash
pnpm --filter @lifebook/api build && node apps/api/scripts/launch-check.cjs
```

**Status on 18 October:** 3 of 9 automated gates pass (API up, sites up, Daily Word from the published translations). 6 manual gates are open.

## Definition of done

All nine automated gates pass in `launch-check`, and every manual gate below is ticked with a name and date.

## Workstreams

### 1. Sign-in and hosting (product owner)
- [ ] **Clerk production instance** for the LifeBook domain, with production keys in Render (`CLERK_SECRET_KEY`, `CLERK_AUTHORIZED_PARTIES`) and Vercel (`NEXT_PUBLIC_CLERK_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`, `CLERK_SECRET_KEY`). Allowed origins and Google sign-in configured. *Gate: `clerk`.* Everything signed-in in production waits on this.
- [ ] **Custom domains** for the app and marketing site, if launching on `lifebook.app`. Update `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL` and Clerk allowed origins.
- [ ] **Hosting plan decided.** The Vercel free plan allows 100 deployments a day (hit twice on 18 October) and is meant for non-commercial personal use. Decide whether LifeBook qualifies or needs Pro. *Manual.*

### 2. Trust, privacy and safety (product owner, adviser, reviewers)
- [ ] **Privacy notice** finalised by the adviser from `docs/PRIVACY_NOTICE_DRAFT.md`, then published at `/privacy` in English and French, replacing the placeholder. *Gate: `privacy`.*
- [ ] **Terms of use** finalised and published at `/terms`.
- [ ] **Data protection review** for Ghana (Data Protection Act 2012, Act 843) and France (GDPR, CNIL guidance). Faith and prayer content is sensitive personal data.
- [ ] **OpenAI:** `OPENAI_API_KEY` on Render as a secret, and the account's data-retention terms confirmed (zero data retention requested if available). *Manual.*
- [ ] **Crisis numbers** verified by a person in each country, and the document's status changed from DRAFT. *Gate: `crisis`.*
- [ ] **`hello@lifebook.app`** is a monitored inbox (it's linked from the site).

### 3. Pastoral content (writers and pastoral reviewer)
- [ ] **Prayer topic library** reviewed: `apps/api/content/prayer-topics.json` → `status: "reviewed"`, reviewer and date. This switches on the Scripture companion and guided meditation. *Gate: `companion`.*
- [ ] **Daily Word: at least 30 reviewed entries**, for the first month after launch (`docs/DAILY_WORD_LIBRARY.md`). Aim for a full year. *Gate: `daily-word`.*
- [ ] **Pastoral care pilot:** pastors registered with `pastor-admin.cjs`, a safeguarding lead given the MODERATOR role, and the notification channel chosen. *Gate: `pastors`.*

### 4. Production data (engineering, each run approved by the product owner)
- [ ] **"Delete my account"** in Settings: removes every LifeBook record for the member (database cascade, including encrypted content) and their Clerk user, with a clear confirmation. GDPR and Act 843 expect erasure on request. Today members can only delete their prayers. *Engineering; manual gate.*
- [ ] **Data export** ("Download my data") for access and portability requests, or a documented manual process the adviser accepts. *Engineering.*
- [ ] **Encrypt legacy reflections.** Daily Word and meditation reflections written before encryption shipped may still be stored as plain text. `reflection-backfill.cjs` deliberately runs only on local restores. Plan: back up, then rehearse on a restore, then run on production with explicit approval, following section A of the runbook. *Manual.*
- [ ] **Schedule the daily care-data purge** (`care-purge.cjs --target production --apply`), which closes the 90-day retention promise. *Manual.*

### 5. Verification (engineering, after workstream 1)
- [ ] **Signed-in production smoke test:** every step in `docs/M3.8_SMOKE_TEST.md` passes with a dedicated production test account. *Manual.*
- [ ] **Real devices:** iPhone Safari and Android Chrome, plus VoiceOver and TalkBack on the main journeys. *Manual.*
- [ ] **Release-day checklist** (runbook section C) followed, and the release noted in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Order of work

1. **Now, in parallel:** Clerk production (1), the hosting decision (1), the adviser starts on the privacy notice (2), writers start Daily Word entries (3), the pastoral reviewer starts on the topic library (3), and the crisis-number calls (2).
2. **Once Clerk production is live:** smoke test (5) and real devices (5).
3. **With approval:** legacy reflection encryption and the purge schedule (4).
4. **Launch** when `launch-check` is all green and the manual gates are ticked.

## Not in M3.8 (next: M3.9)

The iPhone app (PR #37), audio Bibles (waiting on Bible Brain and API.Bible licensing), suggestions v2 (goals and hidden suggestions in the database, "New to this?" notes), and content for the encouragement, discipleship and service goals.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Clerk production setup slips | Everything else can proceed; the smoke test is the only blocked step. |
| Too few reviewed Daily Word entries | The legacy rotation keeps working; launch with at least 30 and keep writing. |
| Vercel deploy limit on release day | Decide the plan first; otherwise batch merges and deploy once. |
| An unverified crisis number shown to someone at risk | The gate blocks launch until each number has been called and confirmed. |
