# Daily Word drafts for November: pastoral review

Thirty **draft** entries for 1 to 30 November, written by engineering on 24 October 2026 for pastoral review. They are not shown to members: production serves only entries marked `reviewed`.

**For each entry, please check:**

- **Faithful:** the explanation says what the passage says, in its context.
- **No overreach:** it doesn't claim God's specific will for the reader or promise outcomes.
- **Fit:** the question, prayer and action suit someone new to faith as well as a long-time believer.
- **French:** natural and correct, and matching the Darby text.

**To approve an entry:** in `apps/api/content/daily-word-library.json`, set its `review.status` to `reviewed`, with `reviewedBy` and `reviewedOn` (YYYY-MM-DD). Write corrections straight into the entry, or add them as notes here. See `docs/DAILY_WORD_LIBRARY.md`.

Scripture below is quoted for review only, from the King James Version and the J.N. Darby Bible (public domain). The library itself never stores Scripture text.
