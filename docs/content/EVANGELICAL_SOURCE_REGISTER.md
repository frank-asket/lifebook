Researched 26 October 2026 from primary ministry/platform pages. **Candidates for review, not approved LifeBook partners or a declaration of permission for every use.** Popularity, an official channel and an embeddable flag do not establish theological suitability or ownership of every recording.

## Credit displayed beside a resource

```text
[Exact work title] — [verified speaker/author]
Published by [ministry] · [language] · [original publication date if verified]
[Exact copyright/attribution required by the rights holder]
Original source: [canonical resource link]
Playback provided by YouTube / original ministry, when applicable.
LifeBook's related questions are original drafts, reviewed separately.
```

An editorial record should retain the canonical URL, exact author/publisher, rights holder if verified, usage mode (link/embed/licensed file), permission reference, restrictions, checked date and reviewer. Unknown rights must remain unknown, not be filled with an assumed ministry copyright. Later publication must also expose the source link and appropriate credit to members; a citation in this developer document alone is insufficient.
